Procedural Cities

A second kind of base: a city grown from a single seed. Avenues wide enough to fly down, a tower core around a central plaza, habitat blocks and quieter outskirts. Every building is solid, and you can still shoot it apart.

What a city is made of

  • Street grid grown outward from a central plaza, with avenues 2.5 times the width a ship needs to fly.
  • Blocks split into lots by alleys exactly one ship-corridor wide, so you can thread between buildings.
  • Districts ringing the plaza: towers at the core, habitat blocks around them, low buildings with yards outside.
  • Buildings built from touching masses, like a tower on a podium, which gives a stepped skyline.
  • No perimeter wall: a city is open from every side.

What we fixed on the way

  • The smallest city size first came out as one lonely plaza block (3 % built up). Small cities now use a smaller plaza and smaller blocks.
  • The tower district first sat inside the plaza, so no towers appeared. Districts are now measured from the plaza's edge.
  • Both are now locked by tests: a building-coverage floor and a "has a spire and towers" check across hundreds of seeds.
