Procedural bases · showcase 2

Base Destruction

Every rack, wall and container can now be shot apart. The hole is real: lasers pass through it, ships fly through it, and a player who joins later sees the same hole.

Seed 42 · 8000 u warehouse · ten ships, one of every flyable type · drawn with the in-game draw plan, piece groups and debris

In the game

What works now

  • Lasers and bolts damage the piece they hit; when it runs out of hit points it breaks.
  • A rack that crosses a map chunk breaks as one piece, not in halves.
  • The server and every player's prediction remove the walls together, so you fly through the gap without a stutter.
  • Debris bursts from the piece's own colours.
  • Late joiners rebuild the same holes from the room state.

How it is built

  • Each broken group sets bits in a small mask (one bit per piece) carried in the room state.
  • Server, prediction and renderer each rebuild from that mask; no geometry is sent.
  • A test-only hit-point dial lets a test break a rack in a few shots.
