# AI pilot dogfighting: strafing a still target

How an AI pilot fights a ship that is parked or slow. The decision layer
(manoeuvres, the utility picker, the virtual pilot) is summarised in
`src/core/CLAUDE.md` ("Dogfight decision layer"); this page holds the story of
the strafing run and why each rule is there.

## The owner's rule

Against a still target an AI pilot flies strafing runs (2026-11-14): "dive in
firing, pass, swing round and come again, like a fighter on a ground target.
Always moving." No Tail, no Hold: both settle beside a stopped ship.

## Still, parked or slow: judged over a moment

`targetParked` (under `PARKED_SPEED_FRAC` 0.1 × my cruise) and `targetSlow`
(under `SLOW_SPEED_FRAC` 0.5 ×) also count a target that was under the bar
within the last `TARGET_STILL_MEMORY_S` (1 s). The brain keeps that memory per
target and fills `TacticalContext.tParkedHeld` / `tSlowHeld`.

Why: in fleet-melee take 6 the scripted pilot looked parked, but the shot's
script (`showcase/shots/fleetMelee.ts`) flies at the nearest robot whenever it
is over 220 u away and then sits facing it. So each time the Robot Orb drew off,
the pilot lurched 40–150 u at it at up to ~220 u/s for ~0.5 s and stopped.
Judged on the instant, each lurch made it a moving ship. The Orb then:

1. broke off its extend (Break: the pilot came at it from behind);
2. tailed it;
3. braked beside it as it stopped;
4. crawled into the next run ~130 u off it.

It never got past ~5 ship lengths and spent 31 % of the take inside 3.

The parked memory also means "it cannot be tailing me" (`isTailed`, the Break
gate). So it only counts once the target has sat under the parked bar for
`TARGET_STILL_MIN_S` (0.5 s). Remembered from the instant, a robot set on a
fighter's six from rest read as parked for its first second, and the fighter
turned to strafe it instead of breaking (`dogfightOnSight`). The filmed pilot
sat still ~0.7–2 s between lurches.

A ship only *remembered* as slow that is on my tail at speed now is fought as
a moving one (no strafing scores; the picker's normal rules, Break
included). Otherwise a tailer that started at half my cruise was strafed
instead of broken from for its first second (`dogfight` "breaks within 1 s").

## The run

`AttackRunManoeuvre` (`src/core/ai/tactics/manoeuvres.ts`) against a slow or
parked ship:

- **Miss.** The pass aims `STRAFE_MISS_OVERSHOOTS` (¼) ×
  `shipTuning.attackRunOvershoot` beside the target, never inside
  `STRAFE_MISS_CLEAR_MUL` (1.25) × `hullClearance`, about 160 u for a robot.
  `hullClearance` (both shield bubbles + one hull radius,
  `tactics/manoeuvre.ts`) is also the rule hull avoidance uses. Once hull
  avoidance counted shield bubbles, the old 110 u miss sat inside its 112–130 u
  clearance. Every pass became a dodge square to the closing velocity, which
  scraped round the target's bubble at 30–80 u/s for ~1 s.
- **Dive.** Until the pass locks (at the pull distance, an overshoot out at
  least), the nose sits on the lead, so the dive fires. Flown along the course
  to the wider miss, the nose sat ~13–22° off the lead, outside the ~14° fire
  cone: 1–11 hits in 30 s instead of 20–30.
- **Pass.** The heading follows the line that grazes the miss circle round
  where the target will be `STRAFE_LEAD_S` (0.5 s) on.
  - It is re-aimed when the target moves so that the line passes inside
    `STRAFE_RELOCK_FRAC` (0.9) × the miss.
  - If the ship is already inside the miss with the target still ahead, the
    pass is over and the extend starts.
  - The `RUN_PASS_MAX_S` timer only ends the pass once the ship is abeam of the
    target, capped at `RUN_STRAFE_PASS_CAP` timers. The pass locks up to an
    overshoot out (440 u for the robots), and a robot at cruise (~130 u/s,
    its boost spent down to the Break reserve) covers ~260 u in the timer, so
    the extend began short of the target.
- **Extend.** The ship flies the locked course, bent to at least
  `STRAFE_EXTEND_OUT` (60° off straight at the target, kept on its sideways
  side) until it is `STRAFE_EXTEND_OVERSHOOTS` × overshoot out. Flown
  straight, a target that moved in front of it turned the extend into a ram
  course: a Robot Cube pressed a nudging pilot's bubble at matched speed for
  3 s, and hull avoidance (relative speed ~0) never reacted. The extend may
  boost down to, not below, the 25 % Break reserve (`BOOST_RESERVE_FRAC`), as
  every manoeuvre does.
- **Swing round.** A new run that starts while still flying away swings round
  in an arc (`swingRound`).

Duels with a moving ship keep the old run unchanged: the point beside the
target, the timer, the straight extend.

## Trigger discipline: fire only when the shot will hit

Owner decision 2026-11-14: pilot skill, not a special power. Shots leave down
the drawn barrel, and a player's fixed guns fire within 0.2 rad
(`FC_MOUNT_FIRE_TOLERANCE`). A nose up to 0.2 rad off the lead point misses a
fighter-sized target at range about 40 % of the time, so an AI pilot with
fixed guns waits until the nose is inside the cone the target's hull covers:

- **The cone** is `hullFireCone(hitRadius + bolt radius, distance to the lead
  point)` = atan(r / d), capped at the 0.2 rad window. `hitRadius` comes from
  `shipAimRadius(kind, shieldUp)` (`combat/shipHitShape.ts`: the shield bubble
  while a non-capital's shield is up, else the kind's hull radius; #18). The
  server fills it on `AiPlayerView` / `AiSwarmView`; a view without it is aimed
  at as the default kind's bubble; a structure gets the plain window.
- **Easing on.** While the nose is held on the lead in fire range, the pilot
  sets `SnapTurnState.aimCone`; inside `easedSnapTurn`'s dead-band an error
  wider than the cone gets a one-tick tap (no minimum hold, no tap back the
  other way within the no-reverse window). The coarse turn discipline is
  unchanged; a nose on the move course, a nudge or a dodge sets no cone.
- **Per barrel.** The shot carries the cone (`AiIntent.fire.cone`), and
  `AiFireResolver.bearingMask` narrows each barrel's window to it, so a turret
  fires only the barrels whose line falls inside the hull's cone, never wider
  than the mount's own window.

Measured, `dogfight-3v3`, 3 × 40 s each (hits = hits on any of the six ships
/ AI bolts fired; TTK = first shield or hull loss to death):

| | hit % | shots/s | damage/s | kills | median TTK |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Before | 53.5 / 58.1 / 57.4 | 6.8–7.6 | 44–52 | 3 / 2 / 3 | 27–32 s |
| After | 91.7 / 92.2 / 87.9 | 5.6–5.7 | 59–63 | 3 / 4 / 3 | 23–27 s |

The kill time from first hit stays well above the 5–12 s aim even at ~90 %
accuracy (damage/s rose ~25 %, but the six ships together land only ~60
damage a second); the cause is not yet measured. Hull and damage are not
retuned for it. Locks: `ai/HostileDroneBehaviour.fireGate.test.ts`,
`tests/unit/aiTurretFacesShot.test.ts`.

## Reference: the combat AI in detail

Moved here from the zone CLAUDE.md files (rules only there). Each entry keeps its locks.

- **NPCs fly with a player's controls only — the virtual pilot (owner rule 2026-11-13, plan composed-harbor §0).** An NPC brain never emits an impulse or an angvel snap: it picks a FACING and a WANTED VELOCITY, and `ai/virtualPilot.ts` (`pilotInputs`, pure, alloc-free, caller-owned `PilotState`) turns them into the keys a player would press — thrust, reverse, turn left/right, boost — as `AiIntent.input` bits, which the worker runs through `applyShipInput` with the NPC's own kind. Handling comes from the kind (`makePilotHandling(kind, shipBodyMass(kind))`): snap turns stop on release; torque (capital) turns stop early on the predicted coast `ω/angularDamping` and counter-turn when even that overshoots; throttle is bang-bang on the velocity error along the nose (reverse brakes; in combat thrust is held by default and snap turns use `easedSnapTurn`, see the Dogfight bullet); `brakingSpeed(h, dist)` is the arrive ramp. `leadTicks: 1` predicts the pose one tick on (the server brain reads the last worker step). Boost is energy-gated with the shared `Energy.ts` helpers and the ship's own pool (`BOOST_TICK_COST`, the kind's `energyMax` / `energyRegenRate`, the same numbers a player's ship of that kind has; server-only state). `HostileDroneBehaviour` flies every mode this way (combat, broadside, guard, patrol, move-to, flock); its decisions (targets, ranges, fire gate) are unchanged. Out of combat it chooses `CALM_CRUISE_FRAC` (0.45) of its cruise speed (the pace the retired `ai.thrust` gave); in combat it has full handling + boost. Difficulty knobs are the pilot's reaction latch / aim, never weaker engines. The worker HOLDS a slot's keys (`aiInputHeld` in `physics/worker.ts`) and re-applies them on every physics step until the next `AI_INPUTS` (cleared by `DESPAWN`, an `AI_INTENT` or a bot warp), like a pilot's held input: the room loop and the worker are not lock-stepped, and keys applied on one step only left most steps unkeyed (slow, barely turning AI pilots on a real server). - **Context steering (`src/core/ai/contextSteering.ts`, 2026-11-13; drives combat movement since slice 2c).** Fray's technique (Game AI Pro 2 ch.18): each agent owns `interest` + `danger` `Float32Array(CONTEXT_SLOTS = 16)` maps (`createContextMaps()`, ONCE per behaviour); writers max-accumulate into them (`addInterestToward`, `addInterestOrbit`, `addDangerFromPoint`, `addDangerFromSegment` for ally fire lanes); `resolveContext(m, out)` masks slots above min danger + `CONTEXT_DANGER_EPSILON`, takes the best interest slot with parabolic sub-slot refinement (masked neighbours count as 0, so it only leans toward safe slots) and returns heading + `speedScale` (0 when every slot is fully dangerous). Slot `i` = ship angle `i·2π/16`, forward `(-sin θ, cos θ)`; `angleForDir` = `atan2(-dx, dy)`. A new concern = a new writer. Alloc-free (#14); callers pass size-dependent radii and lane widths from `shipTuning` (#18). Lock: `contextSteering.test.ts`; bench `benchmarks/ai-context-steering.bench.ts`.

## Morale: when the die is rolled (numbers)

All in `src/core/ai/tactics/morale.ts` and `roleProfiles.ts`; time in 60 Hz game ticks.

- **Triggers** (`MoraleTracker.update`, at most one per decision; priority capital lost, hull 25 %, hull 50 %, burst, outnumbered): hull ≤ 50 % and hull ≤ 25 % of max (each re-arms 10 % above its line; crossing 25 % also spends 50 %); **burst** = hull damage in the last 2 s (120 ticks, 8 buckets) ≥ 20 % of max hull (re-arms below 10 %); **outnumbered** = enemies ≥ 3 × (allies + self) among the ≤ 8 nearest ships within `fireRange × COUNT_RANGE_MUL` (re-arms below 2 ×); the squad's capital died.
- **Lock:** after any roll no trigger is checked for `MORALE_LOCK_TICKS` (300 = 5 s); a trigger that arms meanwhile rolls once the lock ends. FallBack also holds at least that long; Flee holds until the warp.
- **Base weights** (fight on / fall back / flee): scout 0.4 / 0.3 / 0.3; interceptor and fighter 0.6 / 0.25 / 0.15; gunship 0.7 / 0.2 / 0.1; missile boat 0.4 / 0.4 / 0.2; capital 0.9 / 0.08 / 0.02; robot 0.95 / 0.05 / 0; civilian 0.1 / 0.3 / 0.6.
- **Modifiers:** each ally in sensors (cap 6) ×(1 + 0.15) fight on, ×(1 + 0.1) fall back, ÷(1 + 0.2) flee; flee ×(1 + 3 × burst fraction, cap 2); fight on ×(0.25 + 0.75 × hull); enemy summed radii vs own radius: flee ×(1 + 0.5 × log2 ratio) when outsized (ratio cap 16), fight on the same way when bigger; fall back ×2 with a friendly capital near.

**Fleet-melee review, 2026-11-14.** In the filmed take 3 of the 5 fighters the camera followed rolled Flee and warped out within 44 s, with no ship dead. Measured in `showcase-melee` (8 drones vs 10 robots, 44 s, the room test harness): 41 rolls (28 of them burst triggers, most at 100 % hull with the shield still up), fighters 18 rolls with 3 Flee, the scout 1 Flee; 3 of the 4 Flee results came at full hull. Two bugs, both fixed (weights untouched):

1. The damage path fed every hit into the burst window, shield hits included, as a fraction of max HULL: a fighter whose shield soaked a fifth of its hull's worth in 2 s rolled as if holed. A shield absorbs a whole hit (no spill), so only a hull-layer hit is fed now. Lock: `tests/integration/sectorRoom/moraleShieldHits.test.ts`.
2. `MORALE_LOCK_TICKS` was documented as the hold "before another roll" but only a FallBack honoured it: after Fight on, the next trigger rolled at once (one fighter rolled hull 50 %, then outnumbered 0.5 s later, 7 rolls in 44 s), each a fresh chance to flee. Lock: `HostileDroneBehaviour.morale.test.ts` "a Fight-on roll holds".

After the fix, same scene: 19 rolls, the first at 11 s; fighters 2 rolls (0 Flee), the scout 2 Flee (at 75 % and 25 % hull). Still by design and left to the owner's tuning: an "outnumbered" trigger rolls at full hull (a fighter with 3 allies against 5 enemies rolls ~16 % flee), and the size term sums every enemy in sensors (up to 8), not only those attacking.

## Loot pods are not targets

A loot pod (scrap kind 3 drawn as `cargo-pod`, `isLootPod`) is never in the AI target pick (that reads ships and structures), but friendly fire made every AI shot stop on the first thing in its path, pods included: in the filmed 3v3 a robot square firing through a broken-up drone's wreck landed "1" hits on the pod it dropped. Owner: nobody fights a loot pod. AI pilot and turret beams (`SectorRoom.nonPlayerRayHit`) and bolts (`ProjectilePipeline.canHitSwarm`, `shotHitsLootPods` = the owner is a pilot) pass through pods; a pilot's own shot is unchanged, and pickup is unchanged.
