**Status:** idea only. Nothing is being built or filmed for this yet. This file is one ingredient for the melting pot when it is time to make the trailer.

**Where and when:** the owner said this on **Saturday 21 November 2026, about 21:00 GMT**, as one message to Claude in the Claude desktop app (Code tab), working on the EQX Peri repo. ## Summary

A single, mostly unbroken camera move that starts tight on the player's own fighter and keeps pulling out, so each new scale of the game is revealed by the shot before it:

1. **Pilot** — the player leads a V of sub-capitals through a debris field.
2. **Capital combat** — a friendly battleship broadsides a smaller ship and breaks it apart (the break-up mechanic).
3. **Command** — the player drops out of the cockpit into command mode, drag-selects a group and moves it in formation (the RTS layer).
4. **Devastation** — an enemy railgun battleship warps in at the worst moment; each railgun shot goes through one capital after another, and through the sub-caps fighting between them.
5. **Defences** — the railgun ship is erased by several siege-gun hits at once; the camera finds the base that fired them.
6. **Building** — a fast-forwarded build of the base.
7. **Economy, then smash cuts** — a cargo ship leaves through the base's teleporter, ignoring the fight, mines at an asteroid field whose size is then revealed; after that, fast cuts across the rest: gas fields, capitals, territory control, missiles, mines and the other weapons.

The through-line is **scale**: one fighter → a wing → a battle line → a base → a whole region. Each part shows one system working for real, not as a menu.

**Open, by the owner's own words:** the end is fuzzy. Resource gathering must not be boring; the teleporter cargo run is the owner's best idea so far. The final montage list is a list of what to show, not yet a shape.

## Script (draft)

Times are rough, to give a sense of pacing (about 90–120 s in all). "Real game" applies throughout: shots are the game running, not staged cut-scenes (see the *record as is* rule).

### 1. Cold open — the V (0:00–0:15)

- Black, then straight in: **tight on the player's fighter**, the centre point of a **flying V** of sub-capitals. Their wingmen hold station either side.
- They fly through a **debris field**: big broken capital hulls and smaller scrap. The V **weaves through it in formation** — rolling past a torn hull section, threading a gap between two pieces, close enough that junk slides past the camera.
- Keep it zoomed in. The player should not yet know how big this game is.

### 2. The battleship (0:15–0:30)

- The V comes out of the debris **right alongside a friendly battleship**, its hull filling the frame.
- The formation makes a **sharp 90° turn** away. The camera does **not** follow them: it lets them go and **pulls out along the battleship**, showing its length and turrets.
- The battleship **fires a broadside** at a smaller enemy vessel. The shells land and the target **breaks into a few pieces** — the break-up mechanic, front and centre.

### 3. Command mode (0:30–0:45)

- A lull in the fighting. The view **lifts out of the cockpit into command mode**: the player stops flying their own ship.
- Below, a melee of sub-caps fights among the capitals. The player **drag-selects a group** of ships and **orders them somewhere in formation**. The group forms up and moves.
- Short and clear: this beat only needs to show "you can command fleets, not just fly."

### 4. Worst possible moment (0:45–1:00)

- An enemy **railgun battleship warps in**, across the battle line — **crossing the T**, or on a diagonal — at exactly the wrong moment for our side.
- Its **railguns swing round to face** the already-damaged friendly battleship from beat 2, and **fire**.
- The shot **tears straight through** that battleship, **through the one behind it**, and **into a third behind that**, picking off the stray **sub-caps** fighting between them on the way — including, ideally, the group the player just moved in beat 3.
- A few shots like this. Leave absolute devastation: wrecks, broken hulls, debris (the debris the opening flew through came from somewhere).

### 5. Out of nowhere (1:00–1:10)

- Before the shock settles, the railgun battleship is **hit by about five siege-gun shells at once** (the owner said "Carl Gustav guns"; in-game this is almost certainly the **80 cm Siege Gun, "Schwerer Gustav"**, `naval_siege`, from [coastal defences](../features/coastal-defences.md)). It is **obliterated**.
- The camera **pans along the shells' path back to the base** that fired them.

### 6. Build it (1:10–1:20)

- On the base: a **fast-forwarded build** — structures going up piece by piece, showing that you can build bases like this one.

### 7. Meanwhile — the economy (1:20–1:35)

- Switch to **smash cuts** from here.
- A **cargo ship** leaves the base, through its **gates or teleporter**, completely **ignoring the battle** (it doesn't care).
- It **arrives just outside an asteroid field**. It **mines a few pieces** and heads back **off the edge of the frame**, home to the base.
- The camera **pulls out** and keeps going until the **full size of the asteroid field** shows. Scale again, this time for the economy.

### 8. Montage (1:35–end)

Fast cuts, one system each. The owner's list:

- **Gas fields** (nebulae)
- **Capital ships**
- **Territory control**
- **All the weapons**: missiles, mines, and the rest of the arsenal

Then title card.

## Notes for when this is picked up

- **Things this needs from the game** (check each against what exists then, not now): wingmen holding a V on the player while weaving through debris; a 90° formation break; broadside break-up into a few pieces; command-mode drag-select and formation move; railgun shots that **pass through** several hulls and keep going; a warp-in on a chosen line; siege-gun volley hitting one target; a fast-forward of base building; cargo trip through a teleporter to an asteroid field and back.
- **Camera:** most of beats 1–5 want to be one continuous move that zooms out step by step. The cut to smash cuts at beat 7 is the deliberate gear change.
- **Ideas still wanted:** a way to make resource gathering exciting (the owner asked for inspiration here); how the montage ends.
- **Existing rules that apply:** film the real game as it is, never hide UI or re-stage; phone landscape and full art settings for films; stills before filming for look checks.
