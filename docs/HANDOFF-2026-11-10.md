## Decisions (user, 2026-11-10)

**Showcase**
- **Record the game as it is.** Never hide UI, re-stage a shot or change behaviour to make a better video. Fix genuine bugs (stutter, lag, things not firing, bad graphics) on sight; "works, but makes a poor video" goes to the owner as a note. Rule in root CLAUDE.md.
- **The player's view.** Clips film the full HUD (no `cleanworld`). Pilots get the follow camera; pans happen only in spectator mode. No during-take cues that change behaviour (forced reloads, re-sent aggro, keep-alive hull). The recorder enforces the camera rule. Desktop default look: Machined art, new effects, GPU lighting High.
- **Speed-ups are fine** if shown: add an on-screen indicator when the game isn't at normal speed. Slow motion is wanted too. Both feed the shelved whole-game time dilation.

**Lighting**
- High (the desktop default) is now everything-on: long cast shadows. Phones (Medium/Low) are unchanged until a phone run.

**Ranges** (capitals and structures; sub-capitals untouched)
- **Real ranges in the game's km** (1 km = 1,000 u, as the warp map shows): 5" 16.6 km, 8" 25.5, 12" 35, 16" 38.7, Gustav 47, Lindemann 56.
- **Time of flight:** real ÷ 3.
- **No artificial limits:** no base-gap cap, no spread, no 5" restriction. Shells hit anything in their path and explode only on a hit.
- **Lock time** = speed × size × absolute distance for heavy guns. Slow locks are by design. Small targets must be lockable, only slowly: "10 minutes or an hour … working as intended" (**not done yet**, see Next).
- **Picking far targets:** both a contacts list (EVE-style, like the warp list) and a capital zoom out to the longest gun's reach.

**Combat**
- **Friendly fire:** every shot hits the first thing in its path, own side included. NPCs must not fire when an ally blocks (done). Formation spacing comes later.
- **Shields collide as the shield bubble.** The drawn bubble is the collider.
- **The ~0.15 s drone draw delay stays** for now (it explains apparent overlap at speed).
